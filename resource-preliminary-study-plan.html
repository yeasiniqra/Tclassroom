Topper's

Most candidates do not fail the Preliminary because they lack ability — they fail because six months of effort gets spread across the syllabus unevenly. This planner fixes that by putting the marks, the routine and the revision schedule on paper before you start.

What’s inside

  • A full subject-wise mark distribution chart, so study time follows marks, not interest.
  • A printable week-by-week grid for all 26 weeks, ready to fill in.
  • A sample daily routine split into morning, midday, afternoon and evening blocks.
  • A model test tracker to log scores and recurring mistakes.
  • A final 30-day revision checklist.

Who this is for

Anyone starting BCS Preliminary preparation with roughly six months on the clock, whether studying full-time or fitting it around a job. The planner assumes five to six focused hours a day and adjusts easily if you have more or less time.

How to use it

Print the weekly grid and fill in your subjects for the coverage phase first, then use the same sheet to log revision cycles in months four and five. Keep the model test tracker beside you every time you sit a test, and review your mistakes before moving to the next one.
