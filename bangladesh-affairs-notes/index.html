Bangladesh Affairs carries 30 of the 200 Preliminary marks, and the questions cluster around a predictable set of chapters year after year. These notes cut straight to those chapters so revision stays fast in the final weeks.

What’s inside

  • Liberation War timeline and key events, condensed to exam-ready points.
  • The Constitution: articles, amendments and institutions most frequently tested.
  • Physical and economic geography of Bangladesh with quick-reference figures.
  • A running current affairs section updated with the latest exam-relevant developments.
  • A ten-year previous-questions map showing which chapters repeat most.

Who this is for

Preliminary candidates who want a single, exam-focused reference instead of scattered notes from multiple sources. Works well both for a first read and for last-minute revision.

How to use it

Read it once alongside your main textbook during the coverage phase, then rely on it alone for revision in the final month — every section is short enough to review in a single sitting.
