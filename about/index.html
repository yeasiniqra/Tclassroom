About Topper’s Classroom

Preparation That Has a Direction

We aim to help aspirants prepare in a more structured, focused and effective way through quality classes, exam-oriented learning, regular practice, assessment and proper guidance.

We understand that preparing for BCS and other competitive examinations can be challenging. There is a vast syllabus, countless resources and intense competition.

Our goal is to make that journey simpler, more organized and more purposeful, so aspirants can focus on what matters most.

From a Student to a Teacher,
From a Teacher to a Platform

Every journey has a story behind it. The story of Topper’s Classroom began with a journey of learning, discipline and perseverance.

From Barisal Cadet College to Shaheed Suhrawardy Medical College, and later becoming a 45th BCS Cadre Officer, the founder’s own academic and competitive journey helped shape an understanding of what it takes to pursue a demanding goal.

But teaching became an equally important part of that journey. For 10+ years, the founder has been involved in teaching and mentoring students across Udvash & Unmesh Academy and other renowned educational platforms.

During these years, working with thousands of students revealed something important:

Many students have the ability to succeed, but they often struggle to find the right direction, the right resources and a structured way to prepare.

Competitive examinations such as BCS and other Government Jobs demand much more than simply studying hard. Aspirants need to know what to study, how to prioritize, how to practice, how to identify their weaknesses and how to remain consistent throughout the preparation journey.

This experience created the desire to build something more than just another online classroom — a platform where aspirants could find quality classes, exam-focused learning, meaningful practice, assessment and proper guidance in one place. That vision became Topper’s Classroom.

The goal is not simply to help students complete a syllabus. The goal is to help them understand better, prepare smarter and move toward their desired career with confidence.

Topper’s Classroom is the continuation of a journey that began with learning, grew through teaching, and now aims to create a meaningful impact on the preparation journey of the next generation of aspirants.

Learn. Practice. Achieve.

Dr. Mursalin Romans Founder, Topper’s Classroom
